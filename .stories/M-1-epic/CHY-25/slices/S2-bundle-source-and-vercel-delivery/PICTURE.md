## What this slice is about

Bài 1 is served as a real published file, not a test fixture. The app reads it over HTTP, so every lesson route gets the lesson from one load.
Hold this: `lesson/[id]/+layout.ts` → `data.lesson` (`LessonContent`) is the seam every section lane reads.
