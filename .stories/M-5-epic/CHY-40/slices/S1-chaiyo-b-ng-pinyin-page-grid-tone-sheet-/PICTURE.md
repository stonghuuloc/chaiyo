# CHY-40 S1 — picture
ACs: AC1, AC2, AC3, AC4 · Spec rev 1 (advisor r1 applied, r2 ship) · Wave 1 · lane talk

## What this slice is about

A signed-in learner opens **Bảng pinyin**, taps any of 402 syllables and hears its 4 tones in the Yoyo recordings.
Hold this in mind: every real tone plays. "–" appears only for a tone with no recording, which no real cell has (Q1 = A).

| | |
|---|---|
| Repo | chaiyo — new route `src/routes/(app)/pinyin/` + `scripts/pinyin-chart.ts` |
| Lands | table, tone sheet, `?open=` deep link, close + ✕ |
| Does not land | the Nhập môn hub page (CHY-31 / CHY-37) · links INTO `/pinyin` from other screens |
| Seam for other lanes | `resolve('/(app)/pinyin') + '?open=' + encodeURIComponent(syllable)` |

## **AC1** — verbatim

> **AC1** — Scenario: Grid of initials by finals
> When I open "Bảng pinyin"
> Then I see a table with finals as columns and initials as rows, plus a row for no initial (∅)
> And cells that are not a real syllable are empty
> And the table is reference only: no scores

## **AC2** — verbatim

> **AC2** — Scenario: Every cell plays its 4 tones
> When I tap the cell "ba"
> Then a sheet titled "ba" opens with "Chạm một thanh để nghe. Chạm lần nữa để nghe chậm."
> And it lists 4 tones, each with pinyin, a tone contour and a sample hanzi, and each plays on tap (slowly on a second tap)
> And a tone with no sound shows "–" and is disabled

## **AC3** — verbatim

> **AC3** — Scenario: Deep link opens the tone sheet
> When I open `w3-pinyin.html?open=ba`
> Then the tone sheet for "ba" is open
> And with `?open=xyz` the table shows with no sheet open

## **AC4** — verbatim

> **AC4** — Scenario: Close the sheet and go back
> When I tap "Đóng", press Escape or tap outside the sheet
> Then the sheet closes and focus returns to the cell
> And tapping ✕ returns me to the previous screen, or to Nhập môn if I opened the table directly
