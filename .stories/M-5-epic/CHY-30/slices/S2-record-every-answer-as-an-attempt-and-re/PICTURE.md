ACs: AC4, AC5 · Spec rev 1 (advisor 2 rounds: FIX → PASS) · Sub-task: none (backlog repo, no Jira)

## What this slice is about

Every answer in a lesson section is saved once as an Attempt, and a learner who leaves mid-section comes back to the same step.
Hold one thing in mind: callers add ONE prop, `ledger`; the runner finds the resume step itself, and `Ledger.record` now hands back the Study events (S3 uses them).

## **AC4** — verbatim

> **AC4** Scenario: Every answer becomes an Attempt
> *When* I answer any step
> *Then* `Ledger.record` is called once with the question, my pick and whether it was correct
> *And* the screen contains no rule about streaks, unlocks or review scheduling

## **AC5** — verbatim

> **AC5** Scenario: Leaving mid-section resumes at the same step
> *Given* I am on step 4 of a section
> *When* I tap ✕ and later reopen the section
> *Then* I land on step 4 again with the same progress bar
